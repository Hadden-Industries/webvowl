# Historical SDLC records

The repository-owned SDLC was retired in September 2026. Files in this directory,
including adoption and verification records and accepted baseline snapshots, are
historical evidence. Their instructions and activation claims do not govern current
work. Retained plans, designs and evaluations elsewhere also keep their original
scope and results.

Use the [current development guidance](../../README.md#run-locally). Removed policies,
templates and implementation remain available in the
[last revision before removal](https://github.com/Hadden-Industries/webvowl/tree/ff55e522bba36b212cfbdce24cf1468e98eb5c19).
Relative links in original records may refer to files in that historical revision.

Local `.sdlc/runtime/` and `.sdlc/tmp/` evidence is preserved and excluded from Jest
discovery. Retaining those files does not activate a workflow.

## Superseded WP8 adoption request

[Issue #18](https://github.com/Hadden-Industries/webvowl/issues/18) requested selective adoption of source-qualified WP8 controls into WebVOWL's repository-owned SDLC.
That recipient runtime and its trusted-consumer policies were subsequently retired by [PR #23](https://github.com/Hadden-Industries/webvowl/pull/23), merged on 15 September 2026 as [bfcefbde5bd67c9dd93223509919a650aef23fde](https://github.com/Hadden-Industries/webvowl/commit/bfcefbde5bd67c9dd93223509919a650aef23fde).
The adoption request is therefore superseded, rather than a completed or qualified WP8 port.
Resolving it does not reinstall the removed runtime, hooks, schemas or CI gates.

The [original WP8 plan](../plans/2026-09-11-sdlc-wp8-adoption.md), source provenance, accepted baselines and historical verification results retain their original meaning.
Separate product outcomes, ONI host evidence and the combined MCP/SHACL obligation are not completed by this disposition.
An externally selected personal workflow is distinct from the retired repository controls; this archive does not register, configure or activate it.
